ARCVAULT specialises in ultra-luxury residential (villas, penthouses, private estates), premium commercial (office towers, mixed-use complexes, retail), architectural design, turnkey interior fitouts, smart home integration, and comprehensive property consultation across India, UAE, and the UK.

Our residential projects typically start from ₹5 Cr and commercial from ₹50 Cr. We focus on bespoke, high-value builds where our expertise creates maximum impact. For interior design projects, budgets start from ₹75 lakhs for full-home fitouts.

We implement a rigorous 7-layer quality framework — from material sourcing and structural engineering reviews, to BIM-coordinated site management, third-party inspections at 12 key milestones, and a comprehensive 90-day snagging period post-completion. Our ISO 9001 certification underpins every process.

Absolutely. Smart building integration is available as standard on all our projects through our dedicated technology division. We partner with Crestron, Lutron, and Control4 to deliver AI-driven climate, lighting, security, AV, and energy management systems — all unified through a single, intuitive interface.

All ARCVAULT projects come with a 10-year comprehensive structural warranty, 5-year waterproofing guarantee, 3-year MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) warranty, and 1-year full-defects liability period. Our dedicated after-care team is available 365 days a year for any post-handover support.

Begin with a complimentary 60-minute discovery consultation — in person at our Mumbai or Dubai studio, or via secure video call. We'll explore your vision, site parameters, timeline, and budget. Within 5 business days, you'll receive a personalised project brief and indicative proposal from our team.
